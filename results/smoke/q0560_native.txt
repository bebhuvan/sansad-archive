               IMPLEMENTATION OF PMAY-G IN KERALA

    560.   Adv Dean Kuriakose:

                 Will the Minister of RURAL DEVELOPMENT be pleased to state:

    (a)         whether the Central Government will consider to increase the
                Central Government Share of cost of construction of a single
           house under PMAY-G in the State of Kerala, if so, the details
           thereof;
    (b)    the total number of beneficiaries under PMAY-G with a district-
           wise break up thereof in the State of Kerala during the last five
           years; and
    (c)             the details of the total funds sanctioned by the Central
           Government for the implementation of the scheme during the
           last five years?

                        ANSWER
MINISTER OF STATE IN THE MINISTRY OF RURAL DEVELOPMENT
            (DR. CHANDRA SEKHAR PEMMASANI)

    (a):   Under Pradhan   MantriAwasYojana-Gramin (PMAY-G),            unit
    assistance of Rs. 1.20 lakh in plain areas (including Kerala) and Rs.
    1.30 lakh in North Eastern States, Hilly States (including UTs of J&K
    and Ladakh) is provided to the scheme beneficiaries. In addition to
    the unit assistance, the beneficiaries are facilitated with 90/95 man
    days of unskilled labour wages. Support of Rs. 12,000 for
    construction of toilet is also provided through Swachh Bharat
    Mission - Gramin (SBM-G) or any other dedicated source of funding.
    Convergence for piped drinking water, electricity connection, LPG
    gas connection and inclusion of women members of PMAY-G
    households under the fold of Self Help Groups (SHGs) through
    DeendayalAntyodayaYojana -             National Rural Livelihood Mission
    (DAY-NRLM) to explore better livelihood opportunities is also
    undertaken.

     The Union Cabinet has approved the continuation of PMAY-G
till March, 2029 as per the existing unit assistance for construction
of 2 crore more houses. The unit assistance provided to
beneficiaries under PMAY-G is as per the approval of the Union
Cabinet and at present, there is no proposal under consideration in
the Ministry for increasing the unit financial assistance.


    (b):  The district -wise details of targets allocated, sanctioned and
    completed in the State of Kerala during the last five years     under
    PMAY-G is given in Annexure-I.

    (c):    Central share of ₹138.42 cr has been released to the State of
    Kerala during last five years under PMAY-G.
