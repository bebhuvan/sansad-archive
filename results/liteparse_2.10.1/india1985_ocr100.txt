    DISASTROUS WEATHER EVENTS OF 1985


         The  most   striking    disastrous weather events     in   1985  were  the  following  :

         Severe     flooding  in many rivers of the country       during   south-west   monsoon season    took
         a    heavy toll  of   human lives  and live-stock.      It  also   affected  large  areas   of  culti-
         vated   land   damaging   standing kharif  crops   heavily.      The  affect was   rather   severe in
         the    states  of  Haryana,  Punjab and Uttar    Pradesh.

       While some parts experienced floods, concurrently, drought conditions prevailed in
         large   areas   in  Andhra  Pradesh,   Gujrat,  Karnataka,      Maharashtra  and Rajasthan     States.

         A  Bay  cyclone,    during  13-17  October crossed    Orissa    coast,  took a   toll of  57   human
         lives   and   2500  cattle-heads   in  Orissa  and West    Bengal.

         Severe cold     wave conditions    during  first   week  of  January   over  Bihar  claimed    204
         human   lives.

         Severe heat     wave prevailed    over Bihar   during   1st  week  of  June  and  claimed   140  human
         lives.

         Gale   conditions    prevailed over    coastal  areas   of  Kerala   during  4th week  of   June  and
         claimed    18  human lives.    Associated   sea  erosion    caused   extensive  damage   to  agri-
         cultural land.

         The  summary    of  these   natural hazards  is  based   on  the  various   reports in   the   India
    Meteorological  Department   and  the  press information,       The  figures  of  estimated   losses   is
as per the press reports. The areas affected by the weather phenomena are depicted on
    four maps    (seasonwise).
