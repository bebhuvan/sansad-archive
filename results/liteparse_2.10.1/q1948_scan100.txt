    BIOGAS PROJECTS IN RAJASTHAN

    1948. SHRI HARISH CHANDRA MEENA

    Will the Minister      of NEW AND RENEWABLE ENERGY be pleased to state:

    (a) the  details  of the current  capacity of biogas,  bio-CNG and other bio-energy projects     in       the
    country,  State-wise     along   with their contribution     to rural         employment generation and clean
    energy   production;

    (b) the  status   of              units based      on agricultural residue,   cattle dung and bio-waste    in
Rajasthan, district-wise along with the details of the financial assistance provided and
beneficiaries covered therein; and

    (c) the  potential    of the decentralised bio-energy based projects       in  Tonk-Sawai Madhopur Lok Sabha
    Constituency along with the details     of the     progress  made     so far,  role  of local bodies and the future
    action plan made thereof?

    As   per                  the information provided by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA), under
    Swachh Bharat Mission-Urban (SBM-U), the Central Financial Assistance (CFA)                 as   Central Share
    (CS)  funds    are    allocated   to  the   States/UTs for   the   entire Mission        period. CS  funds are
    sanctioned/released      on  the  basis   of demand    made by the     State/UT    in   the form   of complete
    proposals duly approved by State Committees and States     are          then released   to  Urban Local Bodies
    (ULB)    as   per  the  State  Action Plan. Accordingly, the              State Governments/UTs and their ULBs
    implement     the  projects    and action   plans  on ground,   through   sustained     efforts and continuous
    monitoring    of such   plants.
