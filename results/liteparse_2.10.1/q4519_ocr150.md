4519. MS. MAHUA MOITRA Will the Minister of CIVIL AVIATION be pleased to state:-

(a) the total number of complaints received, disposed of and pending since the launch of AirSewa Portal, year and category-wise;

(c) the number of baggage related complaints received against airlines since launch of AirSewa Portal, year and airline-wise?

(b) The Complaints filed on AirSewa Portal is resolved as per Service Level Agreement (SLA). The SLA may be seen at ANNEXURE II.

(c) The number of Baggage related complaint received against airlines since launch of AirSewa Portal may be seen at ANNEXURE III.
